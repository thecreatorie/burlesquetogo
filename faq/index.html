Frequently Asked Questions

◆

How much does a burlesque performance cost?

One dancer performing one song is $150. One dancer performing two songs is $200. A 30 minute show with 4 dancers (2 songs each) is $800. If you hire two or more dancers, they can perform an opening dance at no additional charge.

How do I book a performance?

The fastest way to book is by text: send a message to (702) 205-1440 with your event date and the performer you would like. You can also call the same number or use the booking form on our Book Now page.

Where do performances take place?

Every performance happens at the location of your choice in Las Vegas — private parties, corporate events, bachelorette parties, birthdays, and more.

How long is a performance?

A one-song performance runs 3-5 minutes, a two-song performance runs 8-10 minutes, and our four-dancer show runs a full 30 minutes.

Is burlesque the same as stripping?

No. With burlesque, the aim is to tease and insinuate nudity without exposing it all. In a two-song performance the first song will have top, the second song will not. Burlesque is about storytelling, comedy, sensuality, and anticipation — see our What Is Burlesque? page for the full story.

Do the dancers accept tips?

Yes — dancers accept tips, and it is customary to have small bills to throw during the performance.

Do you have male performers?

Yes! Our Boylesque roster features male burlesque performers Jonny and Urtreen, both highly requested for bachelorette parties.

Can the dancers perform with props?

Our dancers can perform on various props such as chairs, poles, and flow arts apparatuses.

The art of the tease

What is Burlesque?

With burlesque, the aim is to tease and insinuate nudity, without exposing it all. Burlesque is not performed in seedy men’s clubs, but at all kinds of venues and events with a variety of audiences. Burlesque can often be confused with stripping, however the two are very different forms of dance. How so? Continue reading to find out!
